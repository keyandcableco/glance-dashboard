Hey glance

    Type what you'd say: "timer 20 minutes", "five more minutes", "push Sam back to 4", "cancel the bake timer". Common phrasings work at once; looser ones go to Claude Haiku.

    Unread

      Notes

        Pickup

        Alerts now, 10 minutes before, and when due. Clear it from Unread when they've been.

        Staged timer

        Runs on the clock's own timer: the ring counts down each stage and the text line shows its label. Or say "hey glance, focus".

        Focus nudges

        Breaks
        Closing time
        On these days (none checked: every day)

        "Wrap up" 30 minutes before closing, "Closing time" at it. Timers, pickups and hard stops you set aren't affected by any of this.

        Send to clock

        Sound
        Text mode

        The light flash runs all four rings red and white for 3 seconds before the text, as for anything due now. Text mode only applies to text that fits in one piece (about 30 characters); longer text shows each piece once. The play button beside Sound plays it on the clock itself for a few seconds.

        Words on the rings

        Nothing on the rings

        One of the screens in turn, about 15 seconds each time it comes round, with the time and date under it. What you type shows until the time’s up or you clear it; otherwise a pickup or hard stop due within the hour, otherwise the words source’s. With nothing to say, the screen sits out. Letters, digits, spaces and & ! ? - : *, and <3 for a heart. About 6 letters fit on each half; longer lines run down the sides and read sideways.

        Clock settings

        0 follows the light sensor (dim). Higher is a fixed level.

        During quiet hours only critical alerts show. Unread replays are silent and always show.

        Screens

          The clock shows these in turn, about 15 seconds each, and picks the next one itself (often the same one again). The weather is on only a couple of minutes in every ten. A switch takes effect within half a minute, or once an alert that’s showing is over.

          Calendars

            Add a calendar

            In Google Calendar on a computer: Settings, click the calendar, then Integrate calendar. Copy “Secret address in iCal format”. The bridge reads it once to check it, then keeps it on this computer; the panel never shows it again. Each event warns 15 and 5 minutes before and at the start, and goes on the schedule ring.
